BigWorks Social

BigWorks Social is our in-house tool to schedule and publish our own posts and videos to TikTok and other networks. It is used only by the BigWorks team, for accounts we own.

What we do

Our team prepares text, images and videos, then schedules them in BigWorks Social, a self-hosted publishing tool. It connects only to accounts we own, using each platform's official developer APIs (such as the TikTok Content Posting API) with the account owner's consent. Today it publishes Prompt do dia, an editorial brand by BigWorks with daily AI prompt ideas for creators.
